## Highlights

- Added configurable floating combat text, including OpenDyslexic and Segoe UI
  font options, monitor positioning, event spacing, and fade timing controls.
- Replaced packaged command-line OCR with a hash-verified, in-process
  `tesserocr` backend linked to Tesseract 5.5.2, reducing per-pass startup and
  temporary-file work.

## Fixes

- Improved parsing of quoted character names in possessive damage messages.

## Distribution notes

- The Windows installer remains intentionally unsigned and may display an
  Unknown publisher warning. The release includes SHA-256 checksums, a CycloneDX
  SBOM, third-party notices, and build provenance for verification.
